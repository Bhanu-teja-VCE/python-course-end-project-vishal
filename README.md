## Problem Statement
Develop an automotive service CRM in Python to maintain registered customer vehicles, log itemized job cards with labour and spare parts cost breakdown, track service milestones, provide overdue maintenance alerts, and audit garage revenue.

**CO Mapping:** CO3, CO4, CO5  |  **PO Mapping:** PO1, PO2, PO3, PO5

---

## Features
- **Vehicle & Customer Registry:** Registration by license plate number with owner contact details and vehicle model.
- **Itemized Service Job Cards:** Independent tracking of technician labour and spare parts cost components.
- **Chronological Service History:** Vehicle-specific audit log displaying date, odometer, repair description, and cumulative expenditure.
- **Maintenance Schedule Intelligence:** Automatic detection of overdue maintenance dates against the current system date.
- **Financial Analytics:** Gross workshop income calculator across all recorded service visits.
- **Persistent Storage:** Synchronized automatically with `service_data.json`.
