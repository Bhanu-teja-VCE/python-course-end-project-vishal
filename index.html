1. Problem Statement & Project Overview

Independent automotive workshops, garage franchises, and service stations handle numerous jobs daily ranging from routine oil replacements to complex mechanical overhauls. Managing customer profiles, itemizing labour versus replacement parts, tracking odometer milestones, and alerting customers to overdue service intervals with manual logbooks frequently leads to missed revenue and unorganized records.

The Vehicle Service Record Management System engineered by Vishal addresses these operational challenges with a robust, terminal-driven garage management system. The software enforces relational integrity by requiring vehicles to be registered before logging maintenance, itemizes expenditure into distinct labour and spare parts subcomponents, alerts mechanics to overdue periodic maintenance schedules via date comparisons, and aggregates financial gross turnover.

Relational Registration Enforcement

Enforces foreign-key-like constraints: service logs cannot be created for unregistered vehicle registration plates.

Predictive Maintenance Intelligence

Compares recorded future service target dates against current system timestamps, categorizing entries into Upcoming versus OVERDUE.

2. System Architecture & Workflow

The architecture maintains a dual-entity dictionary containing a vehicles registry (keyed by registration number) and a services transaction ledger, persisted into service_data.json.

Vehicle Registration
→
Job Card (Labour + Parts)
→
Schedule Date Verification
→
JSON Garage Ledger

Operational Pipeline:

  1. Registration Stage: Adds vehicle license number as unique dictionary key with customer name, phone number, and vehicle model metadata.
  2. Job Card Generation: Queries vehicle registry. If validated, accepts job description, float-parsed labour charges, float-parsed spare parts costs, odometer reading, and optional next maintenance date.
  3. Billing & Ledgering: Adds labour and parts, logs current ISO date, and saves to services list.
  4. Inspection & Financial Audit: Computes historical expenditure per vehicle and cumulates total revenue across all past service visits.

4. Algorithmic Deep Dive & Underlying Logic

A. Two-Tier Cost Decomposition

To preserve transparent automotive billing, labour and spare parts are collected independently and summed: 

try:
    labour = float(input("Enter labour cost: "))
    parts = float(input("Enter parts cost: "))
except ValueError:
    print("Invalid cost.")
    return

total = labour + parts

B. Date Comparison for Overdue Maintenance

The system leverages lexicographical comparisons on ISO formatted date strings (YYYY-MM-DD) to detect overdue maintenance without complex calendar math: 

today = datetime.now().strftime("%Y-%m-%d")
for s in data["services"]:
    if s["next_due"]:
        status = "OVERDUE" if s["next_due"] < today else "Upcoming"
        print(f"{s['number']} | Due: {s['next_due']} | {status}")

Because ISO format maintains monotonic chronological ordering, "2026-09-01" < "2026-10-05" accurately evaluates to True.
